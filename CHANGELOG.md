# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — First release

Casanova Budget is a private, local-first desktop app for tracking a monthly
household budget. All data stays on your machine.

### Features

- **Dashboard** — income, spending, and remaining budget at a glance, with a
  category breakdown donut and month-pacing bars.
- **Expenses** — add, edit, and delete expenses, each optionally tagged with a
  category and a payment method.
- **Categories & budgets** — organize categories into groups, set monthly
  budgets, and track spending against them with configurable alert thresholds.
- **Budget ledger** — a monthly view of every entry, with per-category and
  per-payment-method totals, exportable to PDF.
- **Year in Review** — year-to-date totals, pacing, and a category breakdown.
- **Settings** — manage income sources, groups, categories, and payment
  methods; back up and restore all data as JSON.
- **Local-first storage** — data is persisted to your platform's app-data
  directory; nothing leaves your machine.
